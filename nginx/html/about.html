About

Northwind Studio is a two-person practice working with memory institutions and independent publishers. We believe software should be legible, quiet, and cheap to run.

We are based nowhere in particular and work remotely across three time zones. Our stack is deliberately boring: static sites, small services, real backups.

How we work

  • Accessibility checked on every release.
  • No dark patterns, no surveillance analytics.
